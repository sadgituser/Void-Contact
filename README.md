# Void-Contact

An AI powered app (ChatGPT-4o) that allows you to talk to the deep and never ending Void!


![Screenshot of every window!](/Assets/photo5.png)



It's _easy to use_, just enter your `API key` for an AI and chat!
There is a (fairly) detailed universe out there for you to explore; and your guide (the Void) is here to help!
If you have any questions, then feel free to **[let me know by email](mailto:kavi@mistrypi.dev)**
